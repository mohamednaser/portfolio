**Okhtein** is a luxury fashion house offering [designer bags, fine jewelry, and accessories](https://eg.okhtein.com/) through its official online store — including collections such as The Equestrian and fine jewelry lines.

## Highlights

- Luxury e-commerce with complex product families (bags, jewelry, accessories)
- International payments and modern checkout (Apple Pay, Google Pay, cards)
- Client-facing brand experience aligned with Okhtein’s craftsmanship positioning
