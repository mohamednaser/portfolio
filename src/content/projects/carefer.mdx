## Overview

**Carefer** automates the full car maintenance journey in Saudi Arabia — from vehicle pickup through workshop work to customer delivery — across multiple stakeholder applications.

## What I built

As senior backend engineer and later technical team lead, I owned platform architecture, complex order status chains, third-party integrations, and team scaling.

## Highlights

- **Multi-application ecosystem** — Admin Dashboard, Customer App, Workshop app, Driver Mobile App, and Companies Portal
- **Workflow engine** — Sophisticated order status chains with automated notifications
- **Operations at scale** — Monitoring, ERP integration (Odoo), and APIs supporting mobile teams
