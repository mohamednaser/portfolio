## Overview

Blog Generator is a **customer-facing AI content product** — generate, manage, and publish blog content with subscription billing and an operator dashboard.

## What I built

End-to-end ownership from product specs through deployment: backend APIs, React frontend, Stripe subscriptions, and AI-assisted generation flows. Every change ships through disciplined SDLC gates (ticket-first work, automated review, explicit merge approval).

## Highlights

- Subscription tiers and billing integrated with Stripe webhooks
- AI detector and content tooling on the roadmap, documented as PRDs
- Production-ready CI with lint, test, build, and security checks on every PR
