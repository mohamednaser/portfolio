## Overview

**Lynk** is an Islamic fintech platform specialising in **Murabaha commodity trading**, enabling Sharia-compliant financing for institutional and retail clients in Saudi Arabia.

## What I lead

End-to-end technical leadership: architecture, team direction, and hands-on delivery on compliance-critical paths — ledger separation, order state machines, exchange connectivity, and tax reporting.

## Highlights

- Financial and operational data stay auditable through deliberate database boundaries
- Commodity order flows scale via queue-driven progression across multiple exchange backends
- Regulatory requirements (ZATCA) are first-class in the integration design, not bolted on later
